 🎮 Game Modes & Interactive Learning

  1. Build-a-Drink Game

  - Drag-and-drop ingredients in the correct order
  - Visual cocktail builder with measurements
  - Time-based challenges
  - Scoring based on accuracy and speed
  - Different difficulty levels (with/without recipes visible)

  2. Speed Round Challenge

  - Rapid-fire ingredient identification
  - Quick recipe recall (30-second challenges)
  - Beat your best time mode
  - Daily speed challenges with leaderboards

  3. Memory Card Match

  - Match cocktail names to ingredients
  - Match drinks to glassware
  - Match garnishes to recipes
  - Progressive difficulty (start with 6 cards, up to 20)

  4. Bartender Rush Mode

  - Simulate busy bar scenarios
  - Multiple "customers" ordering different drinks
  - Time pressure with queue management
  - Combo bonuses for consecutive correct orders
  - Difficulty increases as you progress

  5. Pouring Practice Simulator

  - Virtual pour counter (visual measurement practice)
  - Free pour training with timing feedback
  - Jigger measurement quiz
  - Count-to-pour accuracy challenges

  6. Taste Profile Matcher

  - Match cocktails to flavor profiles
  - "Would you like this?" customer preference game
  - Substitute ingredient suggestions
  - Seasonal drink recommendations practice

  7. Trivia Battle Mode

  - Multiplayer quiz competitions (local or async)
  - Weekly trivia challenges
  - Category-specific battles (whiskey vs vodka, etc.)
  - Timed rounds with point multipliers

  8. Blindfold Challenge

  - Identify drink by ingredients list only (no name)
  - Reverse lookup: given glassware/garnish, guess the drink
  - Missing ingredient puzzle
